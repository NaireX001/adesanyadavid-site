About

The person behindyour book journey.

I’m David Adesanya. I work with authors to develop their ideas, improve their manuscripts, and navigate the process of turning their work into published books.

I believe every author deserves the opportunity to present their work with clarity, care, and confidence, regardless of where they are starting from.

My story

My story

Why I do what I do.

My interest in the publishing world comes from a genuine appreciation for stories, creativity, and the people behind the books we read.

I believe that writing a book should not mean having to navigate every stage of its development and publication alone. There are many important steps between having an idea and putting a finished book into the hands of readers, and I want to make that journey easier for authors.

My focus is on bringing the right support, resources, and expertise together to help authors move forward with confidence.

My background

My background combines technology, entrepreneurship, creative problem-solving, and an interest in building solutions that make people’s lives and work easier.

As an entrepreneur and developer, I’ve developed an appreciation for planning, coordination, problem-solving, and turning ideas into something tangible. I bring that same mindset to the book development and publishing process.

I approach every project with attention to detail, clear communication, and respect for the author’s creative vision.

What shaped my approach

One thing that has shaped my approach is recognizing that a good idea alone does not guarantee a finished product. Every meaningful project requires planning, the right expertise, careful execution, and attention to detail.

I bring this philosophy into my work with authors. I want every writer I work with to understand their options, know what their project needs, and have access to the right support at each stage.

I want authors to have more than someone who simply completes a task. I want them to have a reliable person who understands their goals.

What I believe

01

Your voice stays yours.

Your book should still sound like you. Every recommendation, edit, and improvement should support your original intention rather than replace your voice.

02

A clear process makes a difference.

You should understand what is happening with your book, what has been completed, and what needs to happen next.

03

The goal is a finished book.

A promising idea or polished manuscript is an important achievement. But the journey doesn’t have to end there. I help authors work toward the final goal of publishing and sharing their books.

Beyond the pages

Helping ideas take shape.

My entrepreneurial journey extends beyond publishing. I’m the founder of Doc D Adex Groups and the founder, developer, and CEO of AlertMine.site.

These ventures reflect my broader interest in building meaningful projects, developing solutions, and creating opportunities through innovation.

My work with authors is another expression of that commitment: helping ideas take shape and become something tangible.
