How we work together

Six steps from first conversation to delivery.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your book, your vision, your current progress, and the outcome you want to achieve.

02

Assessment & Project Planning

I assess your project’s requirements, identify the services it needs, and develop a clear roadmap outlining the scope, deliverables, estimated timeline, and costs.

03

Agreement & Project Commencement

Once the scope and terms are agreed upon, we formalize the arrangement, confirm the payment schedule, and establish the project’s milestones.

04

Development & Creative Execution

The work begins. Whether it involves writing, editing, research, design, or other specialist services, each stage is coordinated according to the agreed project plan.

05

Review & Final Preparation

We review the completed work, address agreed revisions, conduct final quality checks, and prepare the necessary files for publication.

06

Publishing & Delivery

Your book moves into the publishing stage. I coordinate the agreed publishing preparation, submission, and delivery requirements so your work is ready for its intended readers.

Every project follows a process suited to its individual requirements.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost to publish a book?+

There is no single price for every book. Costs depend on the manuscript’s condition, the services required, the book’s length, and the level of support involved. After understanding your project, I’ll provide a clear quotation based on its specific needs.

Will I retain ownership of my book?+

Yes. Authors retain ownership of their original manuscripts, copyrights, and creative work. I provide services and coordinate professional support without claiming ownership of an author’s work. Any specific permissions or licensing arrangements will be clearly agreed upon in writing before work begins.

How long does the entire process take?+

The timeline depends on the nature of the project, manuscript length, required services, and revision stages. I’ll provide an estimated timeline and milestones after assessing your book.

Can I work with you from outside Nigeria?+

Absolutely. I’m based in Abeokuta, Nigeria, and work with authors internationally. Projects can be coordinated remotely through online communication and digital collaboration.

Can I hire you for just one service?+

Certainly. You can engage me for a specific service, whether it’s manuscript development, editing, cover design, formatting, or publishing assistance. You don’t have to commit to the entire publishing process.

Can you help me write my book from scratch?+

Yes. Depending on your needs, I can help coordinate book planning, writing guidance, ghostwriting, and co-writing support to develop your ideas into a complete manuscript.

Do I need a finished manuscript before contacting you?+

Not at all. You can approach me with a simple idea, an outline, scattered notes, an incomplete manuscript, or a finished draft. We’ll establish what your project needs and determine the best way forward.

Do you work with all genres?+

I welcome projects across a wide range of genres, including fiction, romance, business, memoir, children’s books, faith-based literature, poetry, self-help, and more. The specific services available will depend on your project’s requirements.

How do I get started?+

Simply reach out through the contact form and tell me about your book. Share what you’re working on, where you currently stand, and what you’d like to achieve. I’ll review your enquiry and get back to you with the appropriate next steps.
